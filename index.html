KNOW YOUR MACHINE

A little seaside know-how.

Spin three reels. Only the centre line pays. Returns below are multiplied by your stake (1, 2 or 5 credits). All credits are fictional and free.

Hang on, or give it a nudge.

HOLD: after some losses, keep up to two reels for your next paid spin. Tap their buttons, then SPIN. Held spins cannot earn another hold or nudge.

NUDGE: two free moves. Tap a reel to move its next symbol up onto the line. The first paying result ends the feature, including a partial cherry return. SPIN forfeits unused nudges and buys a new spin. Skip lets you change stake.

Watch your chips!

Three gulls pay 35× and start Seagull Steal. Five cartons hide prizes of 1×, 2×, 3×, 5× and one gull. Pick once, then collect or risk your bonus pot. The gull takes only the unbanked bonus; your line win is safe. Find all four prizes to collect 11× automatically.

Keep the good times rolling.

Below 1 credit, refill to 100 for free. Your progress saves on this device. There are no purchases or cash prizes. A 0.5× cherry return is less than the spin cost.

Each fresh reel stop is random. Previous results never change your odds. Original seaside art & sounds · Made for a little fun.
